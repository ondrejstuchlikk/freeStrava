    Where do I find the link?

    On a computer, open your activities on strava.com, click one and copy the address bar.

    On a phone, open the same link in Safari or Chrome and log in if asked. Tap an activity and copy the address from the address bar.

    Shortcut: add /export_original to the end of any of your activity addresses and the file downloads straight away.

      Weekly training load

      How is this calculated?

      Every activity gets a training load score, where 100 ≈ one hour at your threshold (your hardest sustainable effort). It comes from your heart rate second by second, or power if you set an FTP, or pace if there’s no heart rate.

      Fitness is your load averaged over ~6 weeks, fatigue over ~1 week, and form is yesterday’s fitness minus fatigue. Set your max and resting heart rate in Settings for more accurate numbers.

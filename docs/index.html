How is this calculated?

Every activity gets a training load score, where 100 ≈ one hour at your threshold (your hardest sustainable effort). It uses power if you set an FTP, otherwise heart rate (Banister TRIMP), otherwise running pace, otherwise a rough estimate from duration.

Fitness is your load averaged over ~6 weeks, fatigue over ~1 week, and form is yesterday’s fitness minus fatigue. Set your max and resting heart rate in Settings for more accurate numbers.
